🌾

Mulching & Soil Cover

Applying 5-10 cm of organic crop residue or agricultural plastic prevents surface evaporation, keeps root zone 3-5°C cooler, and suppresses weed moisture theft.

⏰

Night & Twilight Scheduling

Shifting irrigation cycles to between 8:00 PM and 6:00 AM reduces wind drift and solar evaporation losses by up to 25%, maximizing soil infiltration.

⚖

Managed Deficit Irrigation

Applying sub-maximal water during drought-tolerant vegetative growth stages concentrates water during critical flowering and grain-filling windows without hurting harvest yields.
